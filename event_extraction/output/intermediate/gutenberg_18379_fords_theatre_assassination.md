## CHUNK 9
That night Mr. and Mrs. Lincoln went to the theatre, for the day was not observed as in England.

Some time after 10 o'clock, at a point in the play which it is said no person present could afterwards remember, a shot was heard in the theatre and Abraham Lincoln fell forward upon the front of the box unconscious and dying.

This was John Wilkes Booth, brother of a famous actor then playing "Hamlet" in Boston.

On the night of Good Friday, Abraham Lincoln had been carried still unconscious to a house near the theatre.

At 7.22 on the morning of April 15, Stanton, watching him more closely than the rest, told them what had passed in the words, "Now he belongs to the ages."
