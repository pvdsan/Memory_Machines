## CHUNK 2
The election occurred on the sixth day of November.

Lincoln received 1,866,452 popular votes, and one hundred and eighty electoral votes.

The electoral vote was duly counted in the joint session of the two houses of congress February 13, 1861, and it was officially announced that Abraham Lincoln, having received a majority of the votes of the presidential electors, was duly elected President of the United States for four years, beginning March 4, 1861.
