The awful tragedy in the box makes everything else seem pale and unreal.

Quick death was to come on the central figure of that company--the central figure, we believe, of the great and good men of the century.

The revolver was thrust near to the back of the head of the unsuspecting victim--that kind man who had "never willingly planted a thorn in any man's bosom," who could not bear to witness suffering even in an animal.
